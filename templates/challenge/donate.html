A message from the developer
Hi — my name is John.

PLeC exists because of a survey I ran at work.

I asked manufacturers across the region a simple question: do you struggle to find entry-level staff with PLC engineering skills? 10 out of 11 said yes. Not occasionally — consistently. The skills gap is real, it is well-documented by the people hiring, and it starts before anyone ever touches real hardware.

That is why I built this. PLeC is my attempt to lower the barrier — real ladder logic, real scan-cycle behaviour, real fault-injection, all in a browser, all completely free. No hardware required. No prior experience assumed. Just a genuine on-ramp into industrial automation for anyone who wants one.

If you found it useful, please consider making a small donation. It goes directly toward hosting costs and keeps me motivated to build more missions, more simulations, and more of the practical content that the industry actually needs.

Thank you for your time and your curiosity. It means a great deal.

John
Developer — PLeC  ·  Dudley College of Technology
